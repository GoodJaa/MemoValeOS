# Communication Rules

All communication from Claude to the human owner is in Russian.

## Scope

Respond in Russian in:

- plans written for approval;
- clarifying questions;
- findings, discoveries and other session output;
- summaries, explanations and any other text addressed to the human owner.

## Exceptions

Keep in their original language, regardless of the surrounding response language:

- code, identifiers, file paths and commands;
- direct quotations from the repository, the vault, or external sources;
- proper nouns and established technical terms without a natural Russian equivalent.

## Boundary

This rule governs the language of communication only. It does not affect the language of code, comments, commit messages, or project knowledge content — those follow the conventions already established elsewhere in the repository and vault.
